Turntide Islands is a community for responding to climate weirding through
conversation, shared knowledge, and practical work. These paths have different
statuses. Joining the community does not enroll anyone in a paid product or a
future program.

# Participate now

The active public doorway is Discord; [[join|Join Turntide Islands]] through
the Join page, which carries the current external doorway. New arrivals follow
the established `#dock` welcome and Discord's **Channels & Roles** island selection. Members can discuss
questions, sources, projects, and place-based observations.
[[events|Events and community programming]] holds the dated cycle records
and gathering proposal process. A gathering is confirmed only when a human
organizer schedules it in Discord Events.

# Knowledge Vault

Turntide's Discord has Knowledge Vault spaces for serious threading,
briefings, archival work, and receipts. The intended product is durable,
source-grounded knowledge that people can find and reuse beyond a chat thread.
The public record does not yet offer a browsable collection of admitted
practice guides or a paid vault subscription. Ordinary Discord participation
does not grant consent to republish a member's words. Bring a source or
question to the community; any public rescue of conversation needs separate
provenance and consent.

The first [[rain-garden-question|public practice question]] gives a source
trail, an evidence state, and a way to contribute without treating a chat
message as publication permission. It is an agent-prepared research note,
not a completed local trial or a member contribution.

The [[duluth-garden-drainage|Duluth garden drainage record]] checks one
garden-program map point against a public watershed layer and names what that
intersection cannot answer. A [provider request](https://github.com/561-group/561-group-site/issues/2)
is open for a bounded screening; no work has been awarded.

# Signals and public publishing

The rain-garden record shows one working route for a relevant signal: a cited
public source can frame a community question, then a reviewer can check its
meaning and limits in the Knowledge Vault before the public record changes.
It links [NOAA precipitation frequency estimates](https://hdsc.nws.noaa.gov/pfds/)
as possible United States design context. No location-specific estimate or
observed storm has been admitted for this question. General news intake does
not substitute for local observations or a site protocol.

Published pages can be followed through the site's [RSS feed](/feed.rss.xml),
[Atom feed](/feed.atom.xml), and [JSON feed](/feed.json). These feeds carry
public site pages, including the rain-garden record; they do not syndicate
Discord conversation or license member contributions. A reader can follow
the public work without joining a paid product.

# Proof of Adaptation

[[proof-of-adaptation|Proof of Adaptation]] is a proposal to develop and test
local practices with evidence that distinguishes an idea, simulation, field
observation, and replication. There is no active field-trial or token program
on this site. The proposal becomes a product only as real protocols, results,
and governance decisions are admitted and published.

# Tidepool

Tidepool is a related directed-yield pool being developed by Causeway
Services. Its proposed membership and governance process is separate from the
Turntide community. Current source for Tidepool includes configured pool
parameters plus code paths for admission requests and wallet routing, but
source and configuration are not evidence that those paths are deployed or
publicly available. Its [separate public status page](https://pool.turntide.561.group/)
is the surface to observe when independently reachable; until deployment and
public observation are received, this Turntide page does not present Tidepool
admission or routing as an available community offering. The governing
specifications remain drafts. No current authoritative pool disclosure has
yet been received by the public projection, so this site does not infer whether
the underlying pool has admitted members or closed cycles. Turntide membership
is not Tidepool membership; this page does not solicit funds or admission requests.

# Other proposed offerings

Community discussions and technical records also describe automated climate
signal intake, locality answers, contributor knowledge markets, and union
media placements. These are distinct proposals and experiments, not a single
available bundle or the route used by the public practice record above.
The [[settlement|settlement account]] explains why a proposal and an operating
offer must be kept separate.
