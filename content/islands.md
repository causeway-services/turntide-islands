Turntide Islands lives on Discord as a small archipelago. Every channel is a
place, and each place has its own weather. This chart shows where things are
so you can find your way around before you [[join|come ashore]].

![A hand-drawn chart of the archipelago. The Harbor sits at the bottom center with a dock and a lighthouse. Dotted ferry routes lead from it to the Mainland on the left, Governance Rock at the top, the Knowledge Vault at the top right, the scattered Chatter Isles on the right, and the small School isle at the bottom right.](/images/islands-chart.svg)

# ![](/emoji/anchor.svg) The Harbor

Everyone lands at `#dock`. Discord's onboarding runs there: it asks a few
questions about what brought you and which islands you want to visit, and a
person from the community welcomes you in. Afterwards, `#channel-visas` is
where you change which islands you visit.

# ![](/emoji/islander.svg) The Mainland

The common ground. `#announcements` carries news from the stewards and
`#bulletin-boardwalk` carries everyone else's. `#mainland` is for
introductions and for proposing gatherings. Bring practical and research
questions to `#question-quarry`, mutual aid to `#solidarity-shoals`, and music
to `#soundwaves`. `#workshop` is the voice channel, for when typing is too
slow.

# ![](/emoji/tide.svg) The Chatter Isles

A scatter of small islands, one per interest. Visit any you like and ignore
the rest:

- ![](/emoji/garden_grove.svg) `#garden-grove` for growing things, and
  ![](/emoji/cooking_cove.svg) `#cooking-cove` for eating them
- ![](/emoji/academic_acantilado.svg) `#academic-acantilado` for scholarship,
  and ![](/emoji/analysis_atoll.svg) `#analysis-atoll` for taking things apart
- ![](/emoji/praxis_peninsula.svg) `#praxis-penisula` for theory you can act on
- ![](/emoji/economy_estuary.svg) `#economy-estuary`, `#stock-shoals`, and
  `#trader-trove` for money and markets, and
  ![](/emoji/blockchain_bay.svg) `#blockchain-bay` for ledgers
- ![](/emoji/silicon_sandbar.svg) `#silicon-sandbar` for things that happen
  with and within digital tech, and `#emacs-estuary` and `#gnoponemacs-gnoll`
  for people who live in their editors

# ![](/emoji/vault.svg) The Knowledge Vault

Discord forgets, but the Vault doesn't. `#serious-threading` is for slower
threads and longer arguments. `#vault-briefings` carries briefings on climate
breakdown. ![](/emoji/archive_desk.svg) `#archive-desk` is where useful
conversations get rescued before they scroll away, and
![](/emoji/receipts.svg) `#receipts` keeps the source trails: links,
citations, and evidence. What the Vault keeps is the raw material for this
site's public record.

# ![](/emoji/lighthouse.svg) Governance Rock

The community governs itself in the open. Ideas start in
`#governance-questions` and `#governance-discussion`, become motions in
`#governance-proposals`, and are carried out in `#governance-action`.
`#modlog` keeps moderation accountable. The [[governance|governance record]]
explains which commitments already stand and which are still directions.

# ![](/emoji/sky_desk.svg) The School

A seasonal isle for shared practice, such as the write-for-a-month forum.
Each season leaves its threads behind for whoever comes next.

# Quieter coves

A few closer spaces belong to particular families and peoples. Those spaces
are theirs to open, so they aren't charted here.
